What the book actually is

Not a repair manual.
A reframe.

We all carry emotional damage — heartbreak, failure, loss, the chaos nobody scheduled. The book’s argument is simple: the damage isn’t a defect. Pretending you don’t have any is.

Josias writes with humor, raw honesty, and short exercises. You meet the “Little Me” you left behind. You use laughter as a tool instead of a hideout. You stop waiting to be fixed and start owning the scar.

  • See the hidden value in the damage you already have
  • Reconnect with the younger version of you still running the room
  • Use humor without using it as armor
  • Treat vulnerability as a skill, not a confession booth

Why is the signed copy $20?

That’s the whole price. Book plus shipping. Josias signs it and sends it himself. Amazon is $11.97 if you want Prime instead of a signature.

Do I get a signed copy?

Yes, if you order direct. Josias signs it before it ships. Want a short note in the front? Put it in the order form.

How long does shipping take?

This is not Amazon. He packs them himself. Plan on 7–14 days before it leaves, then 2–4 weeks to a US address. International takes longer. If you need it this week, use Amazon.

What is the Little Me exercise?

It’s the guided exercise from the book and the opening of the Emotional Damage speech. You go back to a memory from around age five to seven and ask that version of you how they are, and what they want. The Short on this page is the one Josias uses in the intro.

How long is it?

89 pages. Built to be finished, not displayed.

Is this therapy?

No. It’s a book. If you need a clinician, get a clinician. This is a reframe with humor and exercises.

Who is it for?

Anyone who keeps living the same year with a new face. Also leaders whose team has learned to manage their mood. If the title made you laugh and then wince, it’s for you.
