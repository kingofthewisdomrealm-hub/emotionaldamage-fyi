What the book actually is

Not a repair manual.
A reframe.

We all carry emotional damage — heartbreak, failure, loss, the chaos nobody scheduled. The book’s argument is simple: the damage isn’t a defect. Pretending you don’t have any is.

Josias writes with humor, raw honesty, and short exercises. You meet the “Little Me” you left behind. You use laughter as a tool instead of a hideout. You stop waiting to be fixed and start owning the scar.

“This book isn’t about fixing yourself. It’s about realizing you were never broken to begin with.”
